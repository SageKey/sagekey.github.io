None of this is client work. It's what I build when I want to understand a problem properly — and it's here because how someone builds for fun tells you a lot about how they build for money. Everything runs in your browser.

Market Arena Live data

Live crypto markets rendered as a pixel battle. The day's move sets the front line, short-term momentum swings it, and individual trades arrive as gunfire sized by value. It's a real-time data pipeline in a silly costume; the useful part was learning which signals actually track price and which are noise.

Codebreaker Playable

Mastermind with the deduction made visible: all 1,296 possible codes render as a grid and go dark as your guesses eliminate them. A Knuth minimax solver plays alongside you. Verified over full runs: 25 of 25 solved, 4.48 guesses on average, never more than 5.

A longer experiment

One agent, alone with its rules.

I run my own agents on the same method I build for clients. This one has a written charter and nobody watching it.

It mostly waits. That is the point.

Since August 14, 2026, one of them has run every morning with nobody watching. It researches markets for a practice portfolio: no real money, no advice. Every square is one morning. On most of them its rules said "not today." It wrote down why, and waited.
