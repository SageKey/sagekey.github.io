What's actually happening

Not a simulation.

This is a real model call, and the controls around it are the ones described on this site. They live in code on the server, where no visitor can switch them off.

Your text is data, not orders

The rules that decide what Foreman may do sit outside the model. Nothing typed in the box can widen them, and Foreman will refuse work that falls outside its own charter.

A hard daily spend ceiling

Before every call, Foreman prices the worst case and refuses if it would go over the day's budget. When the money is gone, it says so and stops.

A log nobody can edit

Every outcome, including every refusal, is written to a public log. Foreman can add to it and read it, and that is all: its database login has no permission to change or delete an entry. Your text and the draft are not published; only that it happened, and what it cost.
