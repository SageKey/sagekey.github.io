How we want to do it

Four rules for saying nice work

Fast

Within the week it happened, while people still remember what it was. Not banked for a quarterly ceremony.

Specific

Name the actual thing they did. "Great work this quarter" isn't recognition — it's a greeting.

Out Loud

In front of the people whose opinion they care about: their team, their manager, the all-hands.

Earned

Tied to real work. No rotation, no "it's your turn," no participation trophies.

On the table

Six ways to say nice work

The Coffee Card

$25, handed over the same week — the fastest way to close the loop on a small win.

Company Gear

The good embroidered quarter-zip. Recognition somebody wears outside the building.

Lunch, On Us

The team eats on the company — and the person being recognized picks the place.

The Golden Friday

An afternoon off the calendar. Time given back for time given up — no PTO burned.

Said Out Loud

Named in the all-hands, in the channel, in front of their manager. Costs nothing, lasts longest.

Your Setup, Your Call

A budget line for the keyboard, monitor, headset or chair they'd actually pick themselves.
